Help & Support

Using Catalogue Search

Find physical books and digital materials

  1. Open Book Catalogue.
  2. Search by title, author, category or keyword.
  3. Use availability filters.
  4. Open a title to view accession and shelf details.

Borrowing Instructions

Select an available title, submit a reservation/issue request, and collect the item at the Circulation Desk. Due dates follow the member category limits.

Contact a Librarian

For account, circulation or digital repository issues, contact the Central Library Help Desk.
