Return Rules

Items must be returned to the Circulation Desk on or before the due date shown in the member's My Books page. Items returned after closing hours may be deposited in the book drop and will be processed on the next working day.

Renewal Rules

A book may be renewed up to two times, provided it has not been reserved by another member. Renewal may be completed through the member portal or at the Circulation Desk.

Reading-Room Rules

  • Maintain silence within the reading hall.
  • Deposit personal belongings at the property counter.
  • Mobile phones must be on silent mode.
  • Food and beverages are not permitted inside.

Digital Resource Rules

Digital repository material is for individual academic use. Bulk downloading or redistribution outside the institution is not permitted. Restricted research material is available only to registered members while on campus.

Lost or Damaged Books

A lost or significantly damaged book must be reported to the Circulation Desk. Replacement with the same edition or current replacement cost plus a ₹100 processing charge may apply.
